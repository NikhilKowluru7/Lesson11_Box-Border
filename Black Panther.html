Black Panther

Type: Variant of a leopard

Where it is found: Tropical rainforests across Africa and Aisa, eg. India, Myanmar and Kenya.

Diet: Wildhog, Deer, Rabbit, Fish, Monkey, Sheep, Goat and etc.

Fun Fact: Black Pathers are actually leopards with a rare genetic mutation called Melanismm which gives them dark, glossy fur.
